## 4. Objection-Handling Lines

Five common objections and the proven responses. These appear in proposals, FAQs, and sales calls.

### Objection 1: *"Isn't this just SEO with a new name?"*

**Response:**
"It overlaps. But the differences matter — and they're measurable. Traditional SEO ranks pages in Google's results. GEO gets your content *quoted* inside the AI answer itself. Schema markup that gives a 1.2× SEO lift gives a 2–3× GEO lift. Headers shaped as questions outperform headers shaped as titles by 20–40% in citation rate. You can win Google and still be invisible to ChatGPT — only 11% of domains are cited by both."

### Objection 2: *"Can't my marketing person / paralegal / junior associate just do this with ChatGPT?"*

**Response:**
"Yes — badly. Most internal teams produce content that ranks but doesn't get cited. The discipline of 'first 200 words must complete the answer,' 'every claim cited and dated,' 'schema validated for 8+ entity types' isn't intuitive. We've spent the last year reading the academic papers and the production patterns. We hand you the methodology, run the build, and train your team to maintain it. Most clients hand it back to us anyway."

### Objection 3: *"How do I measure if this is working?"*

**Response:**
"Three metrics, reported monthly:
1. **Citation count** across 5 engines (we track ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini)
2. **AI-referred sessions** (visitors who arrive via an AI link — measurable in GA4 and via referrer)
3. **AI-attributed leads** (we ask 'how did you hear about us?' on every form; we track 'AI/ChatGPT/Perplexity' as an explicit channel)

We give you a baseline reading at the audit and show you month-over-month change."

### Objection 4: *"Six other agencies have pitched me. What makes you different?"*

**Response:**
"Three things. First, we only work with five verticals — law, CPA, consulting, financial advisors, A&E. We don't dabble. Second, we're based in Providence, which means we sit at your conference table when it matters. Third, we sell a $1,500 productized audit — we don't ask you to commit to a year-long retainer to find out if we're worth it. Take the audit. If we're not, you have a strong artifact and you've spent less than dinner with two partners."

### Objection 5: *"GEO might be a fad — what if AI search doesn't matter in 2 years?"*

**Response:**
"73% of B2B buyers already use AI tools in their research; 37% consult AI before Google. ChatGPT crossed 900M weekly active users this year. The trajectory isn't a fad — it's a generational shift. The only real risk is *which acronym* dominates ('GEO,' 'AEO,' 'AI Search Optimization') — not whether the discipline matters. We design every engagement to age regardless of acronym drift: the underlying skills (citable content, schema, authority signals) work for whatever AI engine wins."

## 8. The "Don't Say" List

Phrases that violate the brand. Never write these:

- "Cutting-edge" / "bleeding edge" / "next-generation" / "game-changing"
- "Unleash" / "supercharge" / "skyrocket" / "explode"
- "We help businesses succeed" (vague-positive)
- "Synergy" / "leverage" (as a verb) / "optimize" (without object)
- "AI-powered" (almost always meaningless)
- "Innovative solutions"
- "Don't get left behind"
- Exclamation marks (in body copy)
- Emojis (in body copy)
- Trademark symbols on words we don't own (don't write "GEO™")
