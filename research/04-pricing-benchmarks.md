## 2. What Professional Services Already Spend on Marketing/SEO

To anchor our pricing in budget reality, we must know what our buyers *already pay*. The "GEO" line item is rarely net-new — it competes with or replaces existing SEO/content/PR spend.

- **Competitive industries / larger sites:** $7,500–$20,000/mo
- **Standard inclusions:** keyword research, technical SEO audits, on-page optimization, off-page link building, content creation, GBP management, monthly reporting

## 3. Audit Pricing — The Key Lead Magnet

Audits are GEO's Trojan horse. The audit decision dictates how the funnel works.

### Market range

- **Productized boutique:** $500–$2,500
- **Mid-market sprint:** $5,000+ (often called "AEO sprint" or "GEO sprint")
- **Free** (lead magnet): increasingly common but devalues the deliverable

### Strategic choice for GEO

Recommend **$1,500 productized GEO Audit** as the primary entry product, with the option to **waive the fee for the first 25 firms in RI/MA** ("founding client" framing) during Months 1–3 to seed case studies.

**Why $1,500:**
- Below the threshold that requires committee approval at most professional services firms
- High enough to signal real expertise (vs. free, which devalues)
- Generates an artifact (the audit deliverable) that the firm can show partners — easing the upsell to retainer
- Recoverable inside the first month of any retainer engagement (trust-building gesture)

**Audit deliverable structure:**
1. Executive summary (1 page) — current AI visibility score, top 3 opportunities
2. Citation gap analysis (multi-engine: ChatGPT / Claude / Perplexity / Google AI Overviews / Gemini)
3. Schema/structured data audit
4. Content citability scoring (against Aggarwal et al. methodology)
5. Competitive citation analysis (which peer firms are getting cited)
6. 30/60/90-day prioritized roadmap
7. ROI projection (visits, citations, leads from AI search)

**Delivery time:** 5 business days from kickoff. Includes 60-min walkthrough call.

**Margin math:** ~6 hours of founder time at $250/hr effective = $1,500 break-even, $0–$500 profit. The audit is sold near cost as a sales tool. Profit comes from the retainer/build conversion.

## 4. Build / Project Pricing

The most common deliverable from Chris's prior work has been a built website + GEO setup. Productizing this is core to monetizing what he's already done.

### Recommended tiering

| Package | What's included | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **GEO Foundation** | Existing-site GEO setup: schema, llms.txt, FAQ blocks on top 10 pages, content rewrite for top 5 pages, JSON-LD validation | **$3,500** |
| **GEO Build** | New 12–15 page Astro/Next.js site, full GEO/AEO infrastructure, 8 launch blog posts, 1 industry deep-dive page, deployed | **$7,500** |
| **GEO Build + Authority** | Above + 5 industry deep-dive pages, 15 blog posts, founder thought-leadership content plan, Reddit/community seeding | **$12,500** |

**Why these prices:**
- $3,500 is below the typical SMB SEO retainer's *first quarter* of spend — easy to approve
- $7,500 anchors against typical web agency project pricing ($5K–$15K) and matches the value buyers are already calibrated to
- $12,500 is the "premium" tier that opens the upsell ladder; positions retainer attachment

### Comparison to competitor builds

- TripleDart "Generative Foundation Engagement": ~$15K (estimated, not public)
- Mid-market "AEO Sprint" pricing: $5K project ([GenOptima 2026](https://www.gen-optima.com/geo/aeo-as-a-service-pricing-models-comparison-2026/))
- Premium agency build: $25K+

GEO's build pricing sits **below mid-market**, which is where it should be in Y1 to remove friction from a market that has not yet been educated to pay $15K+ for "GEO."

## 5. Retainer Pricing

### Recommended tiering

| Package | Hours/mo | What's included | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GEO Monitor** | 4–6 | AI citation tracking, monthly report, 1 content piece, schema maintenance | **$1,500/mo** |
| **GEO Growth** ⭐ | 10–14 | Above + 4 content pieces, FAQ refresh, light authority work (1 Reddit/community piece, 1 byline pitch) | **$2,500/mo** |
| **GEO Authority** | 20–25 | Above + 8 content pieces, founder thought-leadership ghostwriting, podcast/PR placement, monthly strategy call | **$4,500/mo** |

**Tiered annual contracts:**
- Month-to-month: list price
- 6-month commitment: 5% off
- 12-month commitment: 10% off + free audit refresh in Month 6

**Margin math (founder solo, Y1):**
- GEO Monitor: ~5 hr/mo × $250 effective = $1,250 cost; ~17% margin
- GEO Growth: ~12 hr/mo × $250 = $3,000 cost; **slightly negative if rigorously time-tracked.** This is intentional — Y1 retainers are sold near cost to build case studies; profit comes from cross-sell and Y2 price increases.
- GEO Authority: ~22 hr/mo × $250 = $5,500 cost; **negative**. This tier is reserved for high-priority strategic clients who unlock case studies or testimonials worth more than the cash margin.

**Y2 repricing plan:** When 5+ case studies exist, raise tiers ~33%: $2K / $3.5K / $6K. By Y3 with national content authority: $3K / $5K / $9K.

## 6. Hybrid Bundle (the Most Common Sale)

Most GEO clients will buy a **build + retainer hybrid**. The typical sale shape:

```
Audit ($1,500) → Build ($7,500) → Retainer @ $2,500/mo
```

**Total Year 1 client revenue:** $1,500 + $7,500 + (12 × $2,500) = **$39,000**.

**This is the unit economics:** at $39K per hybrid client and a Y1 target of $150–200K revenue, GEO needs **4–5 full hybrid clients** plus some standalone audits/builds. This is a manageable sales target for a founder running solo with warm-market access.

### Bundle discount

Offer **$1,500 audit credit** when client commits to a hybrid (build + retainer) within 30 days of audit delivery. Effective net audit cost = $0. This is the most powerful sales lever — converts audits to hybrids at much higher rates.

## 8. Pricing Psychology — Professional Services Specifics

These buyers have specific pricing reflexes:

1. **Round numbers signal commodity.** $1,500 reads as professionally priced; $1,997 reads as info-product gimmicky. Use round numbers.
2. **Tiered pricing with three options closes more.** Most clients pick the middle (Growth) tier — the [Goldilocks effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compromise_effect). Always present 3 tiers.
3. **Annual contracts win on retention but lose on first-yes.** Default to month-to-month; offer annual discount as the 60-day check-in upsell.
4. **Compare-to-anchor in proposals.** *"You're paying ~$X for SEO already; this replaces nothing — it's the AI layer that didn't exist 2 years ago."*
5. **Scope tightly; avoid hourly billing.** Professional services buyers respect productized pricing because they sell their own work the same way.
