# Competitor Analysis — GEO/AEO Agencies (2026)

**Research date:** 2026-04-25
**Purpose:** Map the competitive landscape, identify positioning whitespace, and locate strategic openings for GEO.

---

## 1. Market Structure

The GEO/AEO agency space is roughly 30–60 firms calling themselves GEO specialists in early 2026, segmented by:

- **Client type:** B2B SaaS (heavy concentration), B2C ecommerce, enterprise, professional services (sparse)
- **Pricing tier:** Boutique ($2–$8K/mo), Mid-market ($5–$15K/mo), Enterprise ($15K–$50K/mo)
- **Geographic focus:** Mostly nationwide/remote; very few have a regional moat
- **Methodology:** Most have a named framework (CITABLE, Relevance Engineering, etc.); a few are still horizontal SEO with a GEO bolt-on

Sources: [TripleDart 15 Best GEO Agencies 2026](https://www.tripledart.com/ai-seo/best-generative-engine-optimization-geo-agencies), [First Page Sage 2026 Top GEO Agencies](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/the-top-generative-engine-optimization-geo-agencies/), [PipeRocket Best GEO Agencies USA 2026](https://piperocket.digital/list/best-geo-agency/).

## 3. Niche / Vertical-Specialist Players (the comp set we should actually study)

These agencies have done what GEO must do — pick a vertical and own it. They are the *model*, not the threat.

### Scott Marketing & Consulting Group

- **Lesson:** Six years old, "youngest" on industry top-agency lists, but vertical depth gives them disproportionate share of voice in their niche
- Source: [Position.digital top GEO agencies 2026](https://www.position.digital/blog/top-geo-agencies/)

### Focus Digital

### e intelligence

- **Weakness:** Too horizontal; "professional service providers" is a category, not an ICP

### Siana Marketing

- **Lesson:** A&E presence is real, validates A&E as part of GEO's ICP

### GreenBanana SEO (Boston-based)

- **Geography:** Boston — closest geographic peer to Providence
- **Lesson:** Boston presence is mature; Providence is genuinely underserved by name

This is the right place for a Year 1 launch — undercuts mid-market on price while delivering most of the value, and matches what professional services SMBs can actually budget. We should plan to move into mid-market pricing ($4K–$8K/mo retainer) by Y2 once we have case studies and the brand has compounded.

## 7. Three Strategic Openings GEO Can Attack

### Opening 1: "GEO for [Professional Services Vertical]" content authority

No competitor has invested in deep, citable content for *"GEO for law firms,"* *"GEO for CPAs,"* *"AEO for financial advisors,"* etc. These queries get search volume from the very people we want to sell to. By writing the canonical 2,500+ word resource for each vertical, we own the AI citation slots for those queries — a self-reinforcing flywheel.

### Opening 2: "Northeast warmth" + boardroom credibility

National GEO firms close on Zoom. RI law firms still buy from people they've shaken hands with — particularly conservative buyers like managing partners aged 50+. In-person credibility (CLE talks, RI Bar events, RISCPA sponsorships, Chamber speaking) is a moat that scales locally to the warm market and nationally as content gets rolled into the brand.

### Opening 3: "Productized audit as Trojan horse"

Most agencies sell only retainers; the boutique market underuses productized audits. A $1,500–$2,500 fixed-fee GEO audit:
- Removes risk from the buyer
- Creates an early commitment (foot-in-the-door)
- Generates an artifact the client can show partners, justifying the upgrade to retainer
- Becomes our top-of-funnel and lead magnet

Discovered Labs and TripleDart sell only mid-market retainers. The audit-led entry path is open.

## 8. Direct-Threat Watch List

These are the agencies that *could* attack our positioning:

1. **Discovered Labs** — could expand from B2B SaaS into professional services (claims it secondary already). Mitigation: outpace them on vertical depth; their CITABLE framework is generic, ours will be vertical-specific.
2. **GreenBanana SEO** (Boston) — geographic neighbor, could roll out a Providence offer. Mitigation: lock RI relationships first via RI Bar / RISCPA / Chamber.
3. **Focus Digital** — already serves solo attorneys + accountants. They are the closest existing comp. Mitigation: their work is horizontal across professional services + trades + medical; we go *deep* on the 5 verticals they spread across.

---

## 9. Implications for GEO's Plan

1. **Pricing:** Launch at boutique ($1.5–$3.5K/mo retainer, $4–$8K builds). Plan to move into mid-market by Y2.
2. **Verticals:** Lock the 5 (Law, CPA, Consulting, Financial Advisors, A&E). Resist temptation to add (medical, trades, real estate) until each of the 5 has 5+ paying clients.
3. **Methodology:** Name a proprietary framework in the brand sub-step. This is essential.
4. **Content:** Ship the 5 industry deep-dives in launch wave; this is where the moat is built.
5. **Geography:** National brand from day 1, but *aggressively* exploit Northeast in-person credibility for Y1 sales.
6. **Productized audit:** Lead with $1.5–$2.5K productized audit; use it as a Trojan horse.
7. **Authority signals:** First Page Sage and iPullRank own thought-leadership credibility through founder presence. Founder content (Chris's bylined posts, talks, podcast appearances) is non-negotiable for credibility.
