## 6. Capacity Constraint Analysis

The model assumes founder + contractors + ops can scale linearly. The real constraint is founder bandwidth, especially in Y1:

- 1 audit = ~6 founder hours
- 1 GEO Build = ~80 founder hours (one-time)
- 1 GEO Foundation = ~30 founder hours (one-time)
- 1 GEO Growth retainer = ~12 founder hours/mo

**Y1 founder hours used in Month 12 (steady state):**
- 5 retainers × 12 hrs = 60 hrs/mo
- ~5 audits × 6 hrs = 30 hrs/mo
- 1 build/mo × 80 hrs = 80 hrs/mo
- **Total: ~170 hrs/mo of delivery work**

This is **above sustainable capacity** for a single founder also doing sales, content, ops. **First contractor hire is essential by Month 12.** Without it, the Base scenario tips into chaos in Q4.

The model assumes contractor onboarding at Month 10–11 to be productive by Month 12. If hire is delayed past Month 14, expect quality issues, churn, and burnout.

## 7. Key Sensitivities

The 3 levers that move the financial picture most:

1. **Audit-to-hybrid conversion rate.** Base = 30%. At 20% conversion, Y1 revenue drops 30%; at 40%, Y1 revenue is up 25%. Levers: tighter ICP qualification before audit, better audit deliverable polish, faster audit-to-proposal turnaround.

2. **Retainer churn rate.** Base = 4%/mo. At 8%/mo, MRR plateaus around $15K instead of growing to $42K by Y2 exit. Levers: monthly value reviews, proactive case-study sharing, founder relationship time.

3. **Average retainer ARPU.** Y1 = $2,500/mo. At $1,800/mo (more clients on Monitor tier), Y2 lands ~25% lower. At $3,200/mo (more on Authority), Y2 lands ~25% higher. Levers: positioning that anchors the Growth tier as "most clients."

**Tax escrow:** 30% of profit set aside quarterly into a separate savings account. Pay quarterly federal + RI state estimated taxes.

## 9. What Would Justify Outside Capital?

This model assumes **no outside capital.** Outside capital should only be considered if:

1. A clear non-organic growth lever appears (e.g., acquiring a complementary regional agency for $200K+ that materially accelerates revenue)
2. The aggressive scenario is being exceeded and capacity hiring lags
3. A major channel partnership requires upfront commitment (e.g., $50K to license/partner with a vertical media outlet for an editorial program)

In the absence of these, **bootstrap.** The unit economics of a vertical-specialist boutique are strong enough that outside capital dilutes more than it accelerates.

---

## 10. Summary

GEO is a high-margin, capital-light services business with an unusually fast path to founder full-time income. Y1 conservative case still produces a livable founder draw ($60K) and demonstrates product-market fit. Base case produces founder income equivalent to a senior corporate salary while building a real asset. Aggressive case produces a $1M+ ARR business by Y3 with realistic effort.

**The largest risk to all three scenarios is execution discipline, not market demand.** Demand is documented; what remains is delivery.
