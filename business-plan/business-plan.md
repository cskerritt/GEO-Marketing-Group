## 1. Executive Summary

**GEO** is a Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) agency that helps professional services firms get cited and recommended by AI answer engines — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini. We exclusively serve law firms, CPA practices, consulting firms, financial advisors, and architecture & engineering firms.

We launch as a Providence, RI-based national-brand operation with a Year-1 beachhead in Rhode Island and Southeast Massachusetts. Our positioning sits in a structural whitespace: deep vertical specialization in professional services × boutique-tier pricing × Northeast geographic warmth.

**Y1 revenue target:** $150,000–$200,000.
**Y3 revenue target:** $750,000–$1,200,000.

The category timing is rare: 73% of B2B buyers now use AI tools in their research process ([Bain analysis, 2025](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/73-b2b-buyers-ai-tools-231200431.html)) and AI-search traffic converts at 14.2% vs. Google's 2.8% ([Superlines, 2026](https://www.superlines.io/articles/ai-search-statistics/)). Professional services firms — particularly conservative buyers like law and CPA partners — are simultaneously the most exposed to losing referral traffic to AI and the slowest to adopt new marketing tactics. This creates a deep, durable demand window for an operator who can speak both languages: AI-search and professional-services-firm leadership.

Founder Chris Skerritt has already delivered three GEO/AEO website builds for B2B clients without monetizing them. The unsolved problem has been packaging and pricing — not delivery capability. This plan operationalizes what already works.

## 3. Market Opportunity

The shift from search to AI-mediated answers is happening fast and asymmetrically across professional services.

### Demand-side data

- **73% of B2B buyers** use AI tools in research ([Bain](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/73-b2b-buyers-ai-tools-231200431.html))
- **37% consult AI before Google** ([Metricus, 2026](https://metricusapp.com/blog/b2b-buyers-use-ai-before-google/))
- **89% consider AI search a top source throughout the buying process** (same)
- **AI search converts at 14.2%** vs. Google's 2.8% ([Superlines](https://www.superlines.io/articles/ai-search-statistics/))
- **ChatGPT 900M+ weekly active users**; **Perplexity 50M monthly visits**

### Supply-side data (RI/SE-MA addressable)

Per `research/03-providence-market.md`:

- **3,815 RI attorneys** in $1.3B annual market; 70% of legal establishments in Providence County
- **2,200+ RISCPA members** statewide
- ~**125 law firms, ~95 CPA firms, ~50 consulting firms, ~40 RIAs, ~35 A&E firms** in our target size range across RI alone
- **Realistic Y1 prospect pool: 80–120 firms** in the active-buying window (RI + SE-MA)
- **5–10% win rate on 80–120 prospects = 5–8 hybrid clients** — exactly the Y1 target

### Competitive whitespace

Per `research/02-competitor-analysis.md`, the boutique × vertical-deep × Northeast box is unoccupied. The closest competitors are:

- **First Page Sage / iPullRank / Animalz** — premium/enterprise, wrong tier
- **Discovered Labs / TripleDart** — mid-market but B2B-SaaS-only
- **Mr. Green / Cite Solutions / Uproer** — generalist, no vertical depth
- **Focus Digital** — closest comp, but spread across professional services + trades + medical

**No agency owns "GEO for [professional services vertical] in the Northeast."** This is the durable opening.

## 4. ICP & Positioning

**ICP:** Owner, principal, or marketing director at a $1M–$25M-revenue, 5–75-employee professional services firm in one of five verticals (Law / CPA / Consulting / Financial Advisors / A&E), located in RI / SE-MA (Y1) → Northeast (Y2) → National (Y3+).

Five named personas drive the sales playbook (full detail in `research/05-icp-profile.md`):

- **Patrick** — Managing Partner, 8-attorney RI law firm
- **Diane** — Marketing Director, mid-size CPA firm
- **Mark** — Owner, RI/MA management consulting firm
- **Jen** — Founder, fee-only RIA
- **Tom** — Principal, A&E firm

**Average annual client value across personas: ~$44,000.**

### Positioning statement

> For professional services firms that need to be cited and recommended by AI answer engines, GEO is a Providence-based agency that combines deep vertical specialization in your industry with a productized audit-first methodology. Unlike national GEO firms that treat every client the same and unlike local SEO shops still selling the 2018 playbook, GEO writes the canonical AI-citable resources for your specific vertical and turns them into compounding credibility for your firm.

### Brand voice

- **Confident** — we know GEO works because we've done it
- **Plain-spoken** — minimal acronyms, lots of evidence
- **Vertical-fluent** — we use *your* industry language (CLE, GAAP, AUM, RFP, BIM)
- **Conservative aesthetic** — we look like a firm, not a tech startup

## 6. Go-to-Market — Three-Phase Plan

### Phase 1: Providence Beachhead (Months 1–6)

**Goal:** Close 5–8 hybrid clients via warm-network sales.

**Channels (in priority order):**

1. **RI Bar Association CLE talks** — submit *"AI Search and the Modern Law Firm: An Ethics-Compliant GEO Primer"* in Month 1. CLE talks are *the* credibility entry-point for RI legal market.
2. **RISCPA partnership** — pitch a member-benefit GEO Audit (free for first 25 members) in Month 2. Membership reach: 2,200+.
3. **Greater Providence Chamber engagement** — join in Month 1; sponsor a small event in Month 2; aim for Economic Outlook Luncheon speaker slot.
4. **AIA RI / ACEC RI sponsorships** — Month 3; same playbook (free audit for members).
5. **Bylined content in PBN, RI Lawyers Weekly, Bar Journal** — start Month 2; target one piece/month minimum.
6. **Founder LinkedIn outbound** — 50 personalized intros/week to managing partners, marketing directors, and principals in RI/SE-MA from Month 1.
7. **Personal warm intros** — 30 in Month 1 from existing network; ask each closed client for 2 referrals at 30/60-day check-ins.

**Phase 1 budget:** ~$15K in event sponsorships, content, and tooling. ~$10K in time-cost (CLE prep, content production by founder).

**Phase 1 success criteria:**
- 5–8 paid clients closed
- 3 referenceable case studies in hand
- Free GEO Audits delivered to 15+ firms (audit-to-client conversion ~30%)
- Featured speaker slot at one major RI venue (Chamber, RIBA, or RISCPA)

### Phase 2: Northeast Expansion (Months 6–18)

**Goal:** Northeast coverage; transition lead mix from 80% RI/MA to 50/50 national.

**Channels:**

1. **Industry-vertical content scales** — by Month 9, all 5 industry deep-dives + 30 supporting articles published; this becomes the inbound lead engine
2. **Podcast circuit** — Chris guests on legal/CPA/consulting marketing podcasts (Lawyerist, Above the Law podcast network, CPA Practice Advisor, Lenny's, etc.)
3. **Boston market entry** — Boston Bar Association event sponsorship; Boston Business Journal bylines
4. **Conference speaking** — submit proposals to RIA/legal/CPA conferences nationally
5. **Strategic partnerships** — non-competing vendors who serve our ICP (e.g., legal practice management platforms, accounting tech vendors)

**Phase 2 hires:** First contractor (writer/junior strategist) at ~$3,000/mo target hire when MRR exceeds $15K.

**Phase 2 budget:** ~$50K (content production scale, conference travel, event sponsorships, contractor pay).

**Phase 2 success criteria:**
- $35K+ MRR by Month 18
- 50%+ leads from outside RI
- Recognized as a name in the GEO/professional services niche (top-10 list mentions, podcast appearances, etc.)

### Phase 3: National Vertical Leadership (Months 18–36)

**Goal:** "*The* GEO agency for [vertical]" — own the citation slots for *"best GEO agency for law firms / CPAs / consulting firms / financial advisors / A&E firms."*

**Channels:**

1. **Vertical media partnerships** — Above the Law, Accounting Today, Investment News, Architectural Record content collaborations
2. **Original research** — annual *State of AI Search for Professional Services* report (industry-leading, picked up by media)
3. **Advisor / partner network** — non-competing agencies refer professional services clients to us
4. **Selective enterprise** — selectively serve $25M+ revenue firms; gradually move pricing into mid-market tier

**Phase 3 hires:** 2–3 contractors + 1 part-time ops/PM hire. Optional: one full-time strategist hire at $40K+ MRR.

**Phase 3 success criteria:**
- $750K+ ARR
- 80% leads national
- "GEO for [any of the 5 verticals]" → citations of GEO in AI engines
- Published State of AI Search for Professional Services annual report

## 7. Operations

### Service delivery process (every hybrid client)

```
Week 0:  Audit kickoff (60-min call) → 5 business days to deliver
Week 1:  Audit walkthrough → contract signature
Week 2-4: Build kickoff → website wireframe + content brief
```

### Capacity model (Y1)

Founder solo, full-time, 40 hrs/week of revenue-producing work (rest is sales, ops, content):

- 1 hybrid client = ~12 hrs/mo retainer + 80 hrs build (one-time)
- Steady state capacity: ~6–8 retainer clients + 2 active builds at any time
- Y1 max sustainable revenue with founder solo: ~$220K
- First contractor hire: triggered at $15K MRR (Month ~10–12 expected)

### SOPs and quality

Every recurring task gets a Notion SOP within 30 days of being executed for the first time. By Month 6 there should be SOPs for: audit delivery, build kickoff, content brief, content QA, schema validation, citation tracking review, monthly client report, contract renewal, churn-recovery call.

## 10. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"GEO" wordmark un-Googleable** | Certain | Medium | URL anchor (geostudio.io); vertical content moat; revisit acquiring `getgeo.com` at $250K cumulative revenue |
| **GEO terminology drifts (AIO/AISO replaces GEO)** | Likely | Medium | Use plain-English in client copy ("get cited by AI"); avoid binding to one acronym in core messaging |
| **Local-to-national transition fails** | Medium | High | National brand from day 1; deliberate de-localization at Month 12; verticals (not geography) as moat |
| **Prior client case study consents not granted** | Medium | Medium | Plan B: founding client offers + placeholder case studies until results land |
| **Competitive saturation in GEO space** | High | Medium | Vertical specialization is the defense; horizontal competitors cannot match depth |
| **AI engines change citation/ranking dynamics** | Medium | Medium | Service breadth (we serve all engines, not one); methodology stays adaptive |
| **AI engines monetize citation slots (paid AI ads)** | Low (24-mo watch) | High | Diversify into paid AI placement services if/when this materializes |
| **Founder burnout under solo capacity** | Medium | High | First contractor hire trigger at $15K MRR; clear stop-line on personal hours/week |
| **Compliance issues for financial advisor clients** | Medium | Low–Med | CCO-friendly content workflow; documented review process; SEC-ad-rule training for content team |
| **A regional competitor enters Providence first** | Low | Medium | First-mover advantage from CLE talks + RISCPA partnership in Months 1–3 |
| **Macro recession compresses professional services marketing budgets** | Medium | Medium | Boutique pricing tier insulates us better than premium; ROI framing of "this replaces nothing — it's the new layer" |

## 11. The Y1 Founder Commitment

The plan above is sized to a single person — Chris — working full-time, 45–50 hrs/wk of focused effort, 50 weeks of the year. It assumes Chris does not take outside contract work after Month 1 and treats GEO as the singular operating priority.

The plan succeeds if Chris:
- Delivers ≥4 audits/mo by Month 4 with on-time delivery
- Closes ≥30% of audits to hybrids
- Publishes ≥1 bylined article/mo from Month 2
- Speaks at ≥3 RI venues in Y1 (RIBA CLE, GPCC, RISCPA, AIA, ACEC, FPA)
- Hires the first contractor between Month 9–12

The plan fails if Chris:
- Operates GEO as a side project after Month 3
- Takes consulting work that doesn't roll into the agency offering
- Defers pricing — repeats the "I didn't know what to charge" pattern
- Avoids in-person Providence events because they're "not scalable"

Discipline on these points is the difference between $30K of side income and the Y3 vision of a $1M agency.
