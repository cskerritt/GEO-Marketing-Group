## Program Description (for CLE catalog)

> AI search engines like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity are increasingly the first stop for clients researching legal counsel. When a prospective client asks AI to recommend a "good business litigation attorney in Rhode Island," the engines answer with a small set of named firms — and the firms that don't appear in those answers are losing pipeline they may not even realize they had.
>
> This 60-minute program walks Rhode Island attorneys through:
>
> - How generative AI engines select which firms to cite (the technical mechanism, in plain language)
> - What measurable factors make a law firm more or less citable
> - How RI Rule 7.1 (Communications Concerning a Lawyer's Services) and related ABA Model Rules apply to optimization work
> - Practical, ethics-compliant steps a RI firm can take in the next 30 days
> - Common pitfalls — including supervision of nonlawyer marketing vendors, false-or-misleading content claims, and trade-name considerations
>
> The session is informational and ethics-grounded; it does not advocate any specific vendor or paid service. Attendees will leave with a one-page checklist of compliant first steps and a written reference memo on Rule 7.1 implications.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this program, attendees will be able to:

1. **Describe** how generative AI engines select citations using Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and why this differs mechanically from Google's search ranking algorithm
2. **Identify** the structural and content factors that make a law firm's online presence more or less likely to be cited by AI
3. **Apply** RI Rule 7.1 and related ABA Model Rules to common AI-search optimization scenarios, including content authorship, vendor supervision, and trade-name use
4. **Evaluate** AI search optimization vendor proposals for compliance and substantive quality
5. **Initiate** a 30-day plan of compliant first steps appropriate to the size and practice mix of their firm

## Outline (50 minutes content + 10 minutes Q&A)

### Part 1 — The Shift (8 minutes)

- The data: 73%, 37%, 89%, and what these numbers mean for legal services buyers
- The mechanics: how RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) differs from Google ranking
- Why "winning Google" no longer guarantees being cited by AI

### Part 2 — How Citations Are Decided (12 minutes)

- The reranker stage and its role in pre-deciding citation slots
- What the major engines weight differently (ChatGPT favors authoritative sources; Perplexity weights recency and Reddit; Claude prizes precision; Google AI Overviews favor multi-modal)
- Why only 11% of domains are cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity
- Concrete examples: live ChatGPT and Perplexity searches for "best [practice area] attorney in Providence" with audience reactions

### Part 3 — The Structural Levers (10 minutes)

- Schema.org structured data and its 2-3× citation weight multiplier
- Question-shaped headers and "front-loaded answers" (Aggarwal et al., Princeton/Georgia Tech 2023, 20–40% relative lift)
- FAQ schema and its disproportionate impact in B2B verticals
- Author bylines and E-E-A-T signals

### Part 4 — Rule 7.1 and Ethics Implications (15 minutes)

- Rule 7.1 prohibition on false or misleading communications, applied to:
  - AI-generated content under a partner's name
  - Performance claims and case-result content
  - Reviews and testimonials in directories AI engines index
  - Trade name and entity coherence considerations
- Rule 5.3 (responsibilities regarding nonlawyer assistants) and supervision of marketing vendors
- Rule 7.2 advertising rule considerations (state-specific RI overlay)
- Multi-jurisdictional considerations for firms admitted in MA, CT, NY
- Documented review workflow recommendations

### Part 5 — The 30-Day Compliant First Steps (5 minutes)

- Take a baseline reading: type your firm name and your top 5 practice-area queries into ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews
- Add LegalService and Person schema to practice-area pages and partner bio pages (no Rule 7.1 issue)
- Add FAQ schema to top 5 pages (no Rule 7.1 issue if FAQ content is accurate and not misleading)
- Restructure top 5 pages so the first 200 words complete the answer to the page's primary query
- Document supervision and review workflow for any nonlawyer marketing assistants or vendors

### Q&A — 10 minutes
